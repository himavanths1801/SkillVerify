Junior Data Analyst - Retail Analytics Team

About the role
You will turn sales and customer data into dashboards and weekly insights for our category managers.

What you must bring (required)
- Strong SQL: joins, window functions and CTEs are required.
- Python (pandas) for cleaning and analysing data is mandatory.
- You must have hands-on experience building dashboards in Tableau.
- Good working knowledge of Excel is required.

Nice to have
- Experience with Looker is a plus.
- Familiarity with Git is a bonus.
- Exposure to Snowflake is preferred.
- Basic knowledge of statistics is a plus.

You will work closely with the business team and present your findings clearly.
