Software Engineer, Platform Team

We are a friendly company looking for a curious engineer who loves solving hard problems.
You are a great communicator, a team player and a fast learner. A bachelor's degree is helpful.

Day to day you will work with Python (advanced), JavaScript (ES6), React and Node.js.
Our data lives in MySQL and MongoDB, and we deploy with Docker on AWS.
We also use HTML/CSS for internal tools, plus Jenkins, Linux and some Rust.

No experience with machine learning is needed, but we like people who have tried TensorFlow.
Strong problem solving skills and 3 years of experience in a similar role are expected.

The team also touches Terraform, Redis and Kafka now and then, though nobody owns them.
Some of us write Go for small command line tools; others prefer shell scripts.
Our internal dashboards are built with a framework called Zorbify, which we will teach you.

Interview process: a short call, a take-home exercise and a conversation with the team.
We value honesty, kindness and clear writing. Relocation help is available.
