Data Analyst, Customer Insights
Northwind Analytics, Bengaluru (hybrid)

About the role
You will turn raw customer and sales data into dashboards and decisions for the product team.

Required skills
- Strong SQL: joins, window functions and query tuning on PostgreSQL
- Python for data analysis, using Pandas and NumPy
- Experience building dashboards in Tableau or Power BI
- Solid statistics: hypothesis testing and regression
- Working knowledge of Git

Nice to have
- Familiarity with Apache Airflow for scheduling pipelines
- Experience with scikit-learn for simple predictive models
- Exposure to AWS (S3 or Redshift) is a plus
- Docker for packaging small data tools

What we offer
Mentoring, a learning budget and flexible hours. You should communicate clearly and enjoy working in a small team.
